How the categories work

Categories follow the 2025 American Heart Association and American College of Cardiology guideline. A reading takes the higher category that either its SYS or its DIA reaches.

  • Normal Under 120 and under 80
  • Elevated 120–129 and under 80
  • Stage 1 130–139 or 80–89
  • Stage 2 140 or more, or 90 or more

European guidelines (ESC 2024) treat a home average of 135/85 or higher as high blood pressure.

For an average you can rely on: sit quietly for 5 minutes with your back supported and arm at heart level. Take 2 readings 1 minute apart, morning and evening, for 7 days, using the same arm and device.

Very high readings: 180/120 or higher. Rest and measure again. Get urgent care if it stays that high or you have chest pain, shortness of breath, a severe headache, weakness or vision changes.
